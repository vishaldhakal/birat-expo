How it works

Most business owners know something is wrong but not where to start. So every visit starts with a diagnosis. You find out where your business stands, then we send you to exactly the desks that can help.

1

Walk in and register

Register at the entry, or online in advance on this page, so your details are ready when you arrive.

Start here
2

Business Clinic

A Business Health Check-up diagnoses your business, and a counsellor sits with you to talk it through.

3

Get your report

You receive a health check-up report that pinpoints your business's main problems and gaps.

4

Go to the right desks

The counsellor tells you which desks to visit: marketing, trademark, legal, Q-HSEF, HR and more.

5

Meet the experts

Talk to service providers on the spot, book a service, and stay connected with CIM after the Expo.

ⓘ
The check-up and the counselling at the Business Clinic are free. Services you choose to take from a provider afterwards are agreed directly between you and that provider.

The desks

The pavilion is round. The BiratBazaar hub stands at the centre, and the service desks sit around it. Here is what you can do at each one.

4

CIM Business ClinicSTART HERE · FREE

Business Health Check-up

Answer a short set of questions about your sales, finance, operations, people and compliance. A report is generated that shows how healthy your business is and where it is weak.

Counselling and referral

A counsellor goes through the report with you, identifies your main pain points, and tells you which desks in the pavilion to visit next.

Laws and problem registration

Look up the laws and rules that apply to your business, and register any problem your business faces so CIM can work on a solution.

Branding & Social Media

Q-HSEFQUALITY STANDARDISATION & CERTIFICATION

  • Check your business against quality, health, safety and environment standards
  • Get a plan to close the gaps
Q-HSEF is CIM's own standardisation tool. It is built for local SMEs, where ISO certification is costly and designed for large firms, and is a first step towards ISO.

Who should come

Small & growing businesses

Owners and managers of SMEs and industries of Koshi Province who want to grow.

Women entrepreneurs

Priority slots at the Business Clinic, women mentors, and help to formalise and brand home-based businesses.

Young entrepreneurs & startups

Founders who need to register, brand, protect or market their idea.

Service providers

Consultants, designers, lawyers, auditors and other BDS providers who want to join the network.
